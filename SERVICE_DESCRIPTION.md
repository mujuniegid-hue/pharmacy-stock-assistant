# Pharmacy Customer Assistant

*A ready-to-run assistant that answers routine pharmacy questions quickly and consistently.*

## The problem
Pharmacy staff answer the same questions all day: opening hours, which services are offered, and what to bring for a prescription. Each answer takes time away from serving customers, and different staff members can give different answers.

## What this does
A chat assistant that answers these routine questions using only the information your pharmacy gives it. It helps your staff respond faster and more consistently.

Example questions it can answer:
- "What time do you open on Sunday?"
- "What do I need to bring for a repeat prescription?"
- "How long does a prescription take?"

## What it does not do
- It does **not** give medical advice or dosage instructions. It refers the customer to the pharmacist.
- It does **not** know your stock. It tells staff to check the shelves or stock system.
- It does **not** guess. If it does not know something, or a service is not on your list, it says it cannot confirm.

## How it works
The assistant runs on a computer at your pharmacy. Staff type the customer's question and read the answer. All your details (name, hours, services, rules) live in two simple files that you can edit in Notepad. No programming is needed to update them.

## What you receive
- The assistant, set up with your pharmacy's name
- A settings file and an information file that you can edit yourself
- A written setup guide with a troubleshooting section
- A "before you go live" checklist
- One setup and testing session at your pharmacy (up to 2 hours)
- A short staff walkthrough (about 30 minutes)

## What you need to provide
- Correct opening hours, services and prescription rules
- A computer with internet access
- A free Gemini API key (I will help you create one)
- Someone at the pharmacy who confirms the information is correct

## Before going live
The assistant repeats exactly what is written in your information file. **The pharmacy owner must check every fact before the assistant is used with customers.** Wrong information in the file means wrong answers.
